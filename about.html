About Fold

Minimal bags, thoughtfully made

Fold was born from a simple belief: your bag should carry more than your belongings — it should carry your story. We set out to create bags that are minimal, elegant, and built to accompany you through every chapter of your day.

Every Fold bag is designed with intention. We pair clean silhouettes with quality materials because we believe in pieces that age gracefully and last for years, not seasons.

We work directly with skilled artisans to bring each design to life, ensuring every stitch and seam meets our standards. From backpacks that carry your workday to crossbodies that accompany your evenings out, each piece is made to move with you — and delivered with cash on delivery across Egypt for a simple, trusted checkout.

Quality Materials

We source the finest leathers, cottons, and natural fibers for every bag.

Timeless Design

Clean lines and enduring silhouettes that transcend seasonal trends.

Built to Last

Reinforced stitching, durable hardware, and materials that age beautifully.
